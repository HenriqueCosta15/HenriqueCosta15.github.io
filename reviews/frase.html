What Is Frase?

Frase is an AI-powered SEO content platform that helps you research, write, and optimize content designed to rank on Google. It analyzes the top search results for any keyword and extracts the topics, headings, questions, and word counts that already-ranking pages cover, then helps you build and write content that competes.

Launched in 2018, Frase has grown into one of the most popular tools for bloggers, content marketers, and small SEO agencies. The core workflow is: enter a keyword → see what ranks → build a brief → write and optimize. Every step happens in one platform without switching tabs.

Where Surfer SEO focuses on granular data density and NLP scoring, Frase emphasizes the research and briefing phase, understanding search intent before writing a single word. The result is content that's actually built around what people are searching for, not just keyword-stuffed after the fact.

Who Is Frase Best For?

Frase is a strong fit if you fall into one of these categories:

  • Bloggers publishing 2-8 articles per month. The Solo or Basic plan covers your volume, and the research alone pays for itself in saved time.
  • Content marketers on a budget. If Surfer SEO's $99/month feels steep, Frase gives you 80% of the functionality at $15/month.
  • Freelance writers doing SEO work. Frase briefs make client deliverables faster and more data-driven.
  • Small SEO agencies. The Team plan with unlimited documents handles high-volume brief generation efficiently.

Frase is probably not the right fit if you're a large agency that needs Surfer's precise NLP scoring, or if you want a dedicated AI writer. Frase's writing quality lags behind Jasper and Claude for long-form content.

Key Features

SERP Research and Analysis

Enter any keyword and Frase instantly pulls the top 20 Google results and extracts their key topics, headings, word counts, and questions answered. You see a complete breakdown of what content ranks and why: domain authority, estimated traffic, word count, and the specific topics each page covers. This intelligence takes hours to gather manually and Frase delivers it in under 30 seconds.

AI Content Briefs

Frase generates detailed content briefs automatically: recommended headings, topics to cover, questions to answer, and target word count based on what's already ranking. A well-structured brief from Frase cuts research time from 2-3 hours to about 10 minutes. The brief updates dynamically as you adjust your target keyword or filter competitors.

Content Editor with Optimization Score

Write directly in Frase's editor and receive a real-time optimization score based on topic coverage compared to top-ranking pages. The score starts low and climbs as you cover more relevant topics. It is a concrete, data-driven signal for when your content is comprehensive enough to compete. Most experienced Frase users aim for a score between 65-80 before publishing.

AI Writing Assistant

Frase includes an AI writer that generates content within the editor, trained to follow the brief structure. You can generate full sections, expand bullet points, or write intro and conclusion paragraphs automatically. The quality is functional for drafts but not polished enough for final publication. Most users use Frase for research and briefing, generate a rough draft with the AI writer, then edit or rewrite in Claude or another dedicated writing tool before returning to Frase to check the optimization score.

Question Research

Frase aggregates questions people ask about any topic from Google's People Also Ask, Reddit, Quora, and other sources. You see the exact questions your audience is typing into Google. Invaluable for FAQ sections, H3 headings, and understanding what gaps your content needs to fill. Content that directly answers real questions consistently outperforms content that guesses at intent.

Topic and Keyword Clustering

Beyond single-keyword briefs, Frase can group related keywords into topic clusters, helping you plan content that covers a subject comprehensively rather than writing one-off articles. For a niche site, this is how you build topical authority over time: a hub article supported by cluster articles that cover every related subtopic.

How to Use Frase: The Basic Workflow

Here's the standard Frase workflow that most content creators follow:

  1. Create a new document. Enter your target keyword. Frase fetches the top 20 SERP results in about 15 seconds.
  2. Review the research panel. Scan competitor headings, topics, word counts, and questions. Identify what most top pages cover and what they're missing.
  3. Build your brief. Drag in the headings and topics you want to cover. Add questions from the research panel as FAQ sections.
  4. Write or generate. Write directly in the editor or use the AI writer to generate a draft. The optimization score updates in real time.
  5. Optimize. Add missing topics from the sidebar until your score hits your target. Then export or copy to your CMS.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Frase good for SEO?

Yes. Frase is specifically designed for SEO content creation. Its SERP analysis and content optimization scoring are built around what Google rewards: comprehensive topic coverage, clear structure, and direct answers to user questions. It won't replace a full SEO strategy, but for on-page content optimization it's one of the best tools available at its price point.

Does Frase have a free plan?

Frase doesn't offer a permanent free plan, but they do offer a $1 trial for 5 days with full access to all features. This is enough time to run 3-4 real articles through the platform and decide whether it fits your workflow.

How many articles can I create with Frase per month?

It depends on your plan. The Solo plan ($15/month) allows 4 documents per month. The Basic plan ($45/month) allows 30 documents. The Team plan ($115/month) is unlimited. "Documents" refers to the keyword research briefs. Once created, you can edit and reuse them.

Is Frase better than ChatGPT for SEO content?

They do different things. ChatGPT generates text; Frase analyzes what already ranks on Google. Most effective SEO writers use both: Frase to research what topics to cover and build a brief, then ChatGPT or Claude to write the actual content, then return to Frase to optimize the final article's score. Frase without an AI writer is faster research. An AI writer without Frase is writing blind.

Can Frase replace Surfer SEO?

For most bloggers and small teams, yes. Frase covers the core SEO content workflow (research, briefing, and optimization scoring) at a fraction of Surfer's price. Surfer has more precise NLP scoring and built-in keyword research, which matters for agencies doing high-volume work. For a solo content creator, Frase is the better value pick.

What's the Frase affiliate program?

Frase runs an affiliate program that pays recurring commissions on referred customers. You can apply through their website. The commission rate and cookie window make it one of the better affiliate programs in the SEO tools niche, especially since Frase's low price point makes it easier to recommend and convert.
