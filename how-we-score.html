How We Score AI Tools

Five criteria. Each scored 1 to 5. No inflation.

The overall score

Every tool is scored across five criteria, each rated 1 to 5. The overall score is a weighted average. Scores below 3.5 are tools with real problems. Scores above 4.5 are genuinely exceptional. Most tools land somewhere in between, and that spread is intentional.

A 4.0 is a good tool worth recommending. A 3.0 is a tool that works but has meaningful limitations worth knowing about. A 5.0 has never been awarded.

What each overall score means

4.5+

Exceptional. A best-in-class tool that's hard to fault at its price point. Fewer than 20% of reviewed tools reach this score.

4.0

Good. Solid tool worth recommending for the right use case. Has real strengths and one or two notable weaknesses.

3.5

Average. Does the job but a competitor probably does it better. Worth trying on a free tier before committing.

3.0

Below average. Meaningful problems in quality, value, or usability. Only right for a narrow use case.

<3.0

Hard to recommend. We still publish these reviews because knowing what to avoid is as useful as knowing what to buy.
