7 Best AI Tools for Podcasters in 2026

Editing, transcription, show notes, voiceover intros: AI handles the parts of podcasting that eat your time so you can focus on the conversation. Here are the tools actually worth using.

  1. Descript: Best all-in-one AI podcast editor
  2. ElevenLabs: Best for AI voiceover intros and outros
  3. Murf AI: Best budget voiceover for podcast ads and promos

Podcasting has a dirty secret: recording is the easy part. Most creators spend 3 to 5 hours per episode on editing, writing show notes, creating clips for social media, and handling transcription. AI has cut that to under an hour for most workflows. Here is what to use and where it fits.

Descript is the most important AI tool a podcaster can use. It transcribes your recording automatically, then lets you edit the audio by editing the text. Descript replaces your editing software, transcription service, and show notes drafting in one tool. The free plan allows 1 hour of transcription per month, which is enough to try it properly.

The AI Podcasting Workflow

Here is how to chain these tools for a complete episode from recording to publishing:

Before recording: Use ChatGPT to plan your episode structure and generate interview questions. 15 minutes of prep produces better conversations than winging it.

Recording: Use Riverside.fm for remote guests. For solo episodes, any decent USB microphone works fine.

Post-production: Import into Descript. Remove filler words in one click. Edit by cutting from the transcript. Add studio sound enhancement. Generate captions if you are also putting the episode on YouTube.

Show notes and social: Export the transcript from Descript, paste into ChatGPT, generate show notes, title options, timestamps, and 3 social media posts.

Intros and outros: Record them yourself, or use ElevenLabs to generate a polished narrated intro from a script in your voice.

A 45-minute episode that used to take 4 hours of post-production can be done in under 90 minutes with this stack. The free tiers of Descript, ChatGPT, Adobe Podcast, and Otter.ai cover the entire workflow before you spend a dollar.

Do I Need All of These?

No. Start with just two: Descript and ChatGPT. Descript handles editing, transcription, and filler word removal. ChatGPT handles show notes and titles. Those two tools alone will save you 2 to 3 hours per episode. Add ElevenLabs when you want a polished AI intro, and Riverside when you start recording remote guests.
