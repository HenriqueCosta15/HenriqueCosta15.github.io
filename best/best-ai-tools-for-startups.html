How to Build Your Startup AI Stack

The right stack depends on your stage and your biggest bottleneck:

Pre-product / idea stage: ChatGPT for everything. Validate your idea, research the market, draft your pitch, write your landing page. Cost: $0.

Building the product: Add Cursor for faster engineering. Add Notion AI for documentation as the team grows. Cost: $30 to $40/month.

Early go-to-market: Add Rytr for marketing copy and Canva for design assets. Add ElevenLabs when you need video narration. Cost: $30 to $50/month additional.

Scaling: Add Synthesia for video at scale, QuillBot for communications quality. Consider Jasper when you need brand-consistent copy across a larger team. Cost scales with headcount and output volume.

The total cost of a startup AI stack that replaces a content writer, a designer, and partially a developer is under $150/month. That is a fraction of one hire.
