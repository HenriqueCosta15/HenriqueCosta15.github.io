The PM Stack

You Don't Need All Seven

The highest-impact AI stack for most project managers is three tools:

  • Otter AI: Free plan covers most PMs. Eliminate post-meeting documentation entirely. This is the single highest-ROI change most PMs can make.
  • ChatGPT: Free or $20/month. Handles status reports, stakeholder emails, risk logs, and every other written deliverable. One prompt, one draft, light editing, done.
  • Grammarly Free: Runs in the background across every tool you use. No reason not to have it.

Add Notion AI if your team already lives in Notion. Add QuillBot if you regularly translate technical output for business audiences. Skip the rest until you have a specific gap.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can AI help project managers?

AI helps project managers most in three areas: meeting documentation (Otter AI transcribes and summarizes every meeting automatically), written communication (ChatGPT and Grammarly speed up status reports, stakeholder emails, and project briefs), and documentation management (Notion AI organizes project knowledge and drafts from bullet points). The biggest time saving comes from eliminating the post-meeting documentation burden.

What is the best AI tool for meeting notes?

Otter AI is the strongest option for project managers. It joins meetings automatically, transcribes in real time with speaker labels, extracts action items, and generates a summary. The transcript is searchable, which means you can find any decision or commitment from any past meeting in seconds. The free plan covers a solid amount of monthly transcription.

Can AI write project status reports?

Yes. ChatGPT can draft complete status reports from a bullet-point summary of progress, blockers, and upcoming milestones. Give it your reporting template and it will fill it out consistently every time. Notion AI does the same inside your workspace. Most PMs find AI cuts status report writing from 30 minutes to under 5.
