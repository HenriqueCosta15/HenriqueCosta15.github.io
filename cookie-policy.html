What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how you use the site.

What Cookies We Use

Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics): We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site — which pages are visited, how long people stay, and where traffic comes from. This data is anonymized and helps us improve the site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information.

Affiliate Tracking Cookies: When you click an affiliate link on our site, affiliate programs (such as Impact, ShareASale, and others) set cookies to track whether you complete a purchase. This is how we earn commissions. These cookies are set by third parties and governed by their own privacy policies.

Preference Cookies: We may use cookies to remember your cookie consent choice so we don't ask you repeatedly.

Your Choices

You can accept or decline non-essential cookies using the cookie consent banner when you first visit our site. You can also change your browser settings to refuse cookies — note that some site features may not work correctly if you do.

To opt out of Google Analytics tracking specifically, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Third-Party Cookies

Affiliate programs and advertising networks we work with may set their own cookies. We do not control these cookies. Please refer to the privacy policies of those services for more information.
