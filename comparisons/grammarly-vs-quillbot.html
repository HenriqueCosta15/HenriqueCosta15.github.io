Grammarly vs QuillBot 2026: Which Writing Tool Should You Use?

Two of the most popular writing tools — but they're built for different jobs. Here's exactly when to use each, and whether you need both.

Quick Answer

Choose Grammarly if: you want a comprehensive writing assistant that improves grammar, style, tone, and clarity — and follows you everywhere you write, from email to Google Docs to Slack.

Choose QuillBot if: your primary need is paraphrasing — rewriting sentences and paragraphs in different ways while keeping the meaning. It's the best paraphrasing tool available, and students in particular rely on it for working with source material.

Use both if: you're a student or researcher who needs QuillBot's paraphrasing for summarizing sources and Grammarly's grammar checking for polishing final drafts. Many people run both at no cost using their free plans.

Grammar and Style Checking

Grammarly is in a different category for grammar checking. It catches errors that most people miss — misplaced modifiers, subject-verb agreement failures in complex sentences, comma splice issues, passive voice overuse, and subtle clarity problems that make writing harder to read without technically being "wrong." The Premium version adds style suggestions and tone analysis that genuinely improve the quality of your writing over time.

QuillBot's grammar checking is functional but basic — it catches obvious spelling errors and common mistakes, but misses the subtler issues that Grammarly flags. If grammar accuracy is your goal, there's no comparison.

Paraphrasing: QuillBot's Strength

QuillBot is the best paraphrasing tool available. It offers eight different modes — Standard, Fluency, Creative, Formal, Academic, Simple, Expand, and Shorten — each producing genuinely different rewrites that preserve the original meaning. You can dial in exactly the tone and length you need.

The quality is impressive. QuillBot doesn't just swap synonyms — it restructures sentences, changes phrasing, and produces output that reads naturally in the target style. Students use it to understand and rephrase source material; professionals use it to rewrite content for different audiences. Grammarly's rephrasing feature exists but feels rudimentary in comparison.

Grammarly's Browser Extension: The Real Advantage

Grammarly's browser extension works across almost every website — Gmail, Google Docs, LinkedIn, Twitter, Slack, Notion, and more. Once installed, it silently checks everything you type in real time. For people who write across many different tools throughout the day, this is enormously convenient.

QuillBot's extension is more limited — it works well on the QuillBot website itself and a smaller range of other platforms. If you want passive grammar checking that follows you everywhere without thinking about it, Grammarly's extension is one of the best-designed tools in this space.

Academic Tools: QuillBot's Edge for Students

Beyond paraphrasing, QuillBot has features specifically designed for academic work: a summarizer that condenses long papers into key points, a citation generator that produces properly formatted references (APA, MLA, Chicago), and a grammar checker calibrated for academic writing. For students, these features together make QuillBot a one-stop shop for research-based work.

Grammarly has a plagiarism checker (Premium) and some academic-style suggestions, but it lacks the citation generator and summarizer that make QuillBot specifically useful for academic writing workflows.

AI Writing Generation

Grammarly has pushed further into AI writing with Grammarly GO — you can generate drafts, rewrite sections, adjust tone on command, and get suggestions for improving entire paragraphs. It's not as powerful as a dedicated AI writer like Jasper or ChatGPT, but for someone already using Grammarly for editing, having some generation capability built in is useful.

QuillBot's generation features are minimal. It's not designed to be an AI writer — it's designed to rewrite and improve what you've already written.

Pricing Comparison

QuillBot Premium at $8.33/month (billed annually) is notably cheaper than Grammarly Premium at $12/month. Both have free plans that are genuinely useful — Grammarly's free plan handles basic grammar, QuillBot's free plan gives limited paraphrasing. For most users, the free plans of both tools together cover a lot of ground without spending anything.

Verdict: Grammarly Wins Overall, QuillBot Wins for Paraphrasing

If you can only pick one, pick Grammarly. It's the more versatile tool — it improves your writing across every surface you write on, catches more errors, and with Grammarly GO adds generation capability. The browser extension alone makes it worth having.

But QuillBot isn't a fallback — it's the best paraphrasing tool there is, and for students in particular it's indispensable. The good news: both have free plans strong enough that you don't have to choose. Use QuillBot for source material and paraphrasing; use Grammarly to polish the final output. The combination costs nothing to try.
