A Note on Academic Integrity

AI tools are powerful study aids but should be used responsibly. Use them to understand material better, improve your writing, and organize your thoughts — not to submit AI-generated work as your own. Most universities now have clear AI use policies. When in doubt, ask your professor what's permitted.
