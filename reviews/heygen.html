What Is HeyGen?

HeyGen is an AI video generation platform that lets you create professional talking-head videos using AI avatars. But HeyGen has carved out a distinct position: better pricing for individual creators, a standout video translation feature, and avatar quality that has closed the gap with more expensive competitors.

Founded in 2020, HeyGen has grown rapidly and is now used by over 40,000 businesses. It's particularly popular with content creators building faceless YouTube channels, marketing teams producing explainer videos, and businesses that need to localize content for international markets.

Key Features

100+ AI Avatars

HeyGen offers 100+ pre-built AI avatars covering a wide range of ethnicities, ages, and presentation styles. Avatar quality is strong: lip sync is accurate, expressions are natural, and the overall realism holds up well for business and content creator use cases. While Synthesia's avatars still have a slight edge in subtle facial movement, HeyGen's are fully competitive for 95% of use cases.

Video Translation with Lip Sync

This is HeyGen's killer feature. Upload any video in English and HeyGen translates it into 40+ languages with lip sync that matches the speaker's mouth movements to the translated audio. The result looks native, not dubbed. For businesses wanting to reach international audiences with existing video content, this is genuinely impressive technology with no real equivalent at this price point.

Custom Avatar Creation

Record a 2-minute video of yourself and HeyGen generates a custom AI avatar that speaks your scripts in your voice and likeness. Available on the Creator plan ($29/month), which is significantly more accessible than Synthesia's $89/month requirement. Quality is very good for business use, making personal brand video content sustainable at scale.

HeyGen's Instant Avatar creates a usable custom avatar from just 30 seconds of footage. Quality is lower than the full custom process, but for quick tests or informal content it works. A useful feature for creators who want to preview custom avatar quality before committing to a full recording session.

Video Speed

HeyGen generates videos in 1-2 minutes on average, faster than Synthesia's 3-5 minute generation time. For high-volume content production this matters. For occasional use it's a minor convenience.

Who Should Use HeyGen?
