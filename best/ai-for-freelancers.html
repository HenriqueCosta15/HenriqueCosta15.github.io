How to Choose

If you're new to AI tools and don't know where to start, start with Claude (free) and Grammarly (free). Those two alone will save time on the most common freelance tasks: writing and communication. Add Otter.ai if you have frequent client calls, and Canva if you produce visual deliverables.

Don't pay for everything at once. Add tools one at a time as you hit the limit of what the free versions can do. The best freelance AI stack is the one you actually use every day, not the one with the most features.
