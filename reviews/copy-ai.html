What Is Copy.ai?

Copy.ai is an AI content platform that specializes in marketing copy and automated content workflows. Unlike general AI writers, Copy.ai is built around "Workflows" — automated pipelines that take an input (a product name, a URL, a brief) and produce multiple content pieces simultaneously.

With over 10 million users, it's one of the most adopted AI writing tools in the market, particularly popular among sales teams, e-commerce brands, and marketing agencies.

Key Features

Workflows (Automation)

This is Copy.ai's flagship feature and true differentiator. Build a workflow that takes a product description and automatically generates: an email sequence, LinkedIn posts, ad copy, and a blog outline — all in one run. Once built, workflows run with one click. Agencies use this to scale content production without scaling headcount.

Chat Interface

A ChatGPT-style interface with marketing-specific presets. Ask it to write a cold email, a product page, or a sales script and it delivers formatted, ready-to-use output. Noticeably better than generic AI tools for persuasive copy.

Templates Library

90+ templates covering every marketing format: AIDA emails, PAS frameworks, Facebook ads, product descriptions, LinkedIn posts, YouTube descriptions, and more. Good starting points that speed up production significantly.

Brand Voice

Train Copy.ai on your brand's tone and language. Once configured, all outputs match your style without manual adjustment. Less sophisticated than Jasper's brand voice but functional for most use cases.
