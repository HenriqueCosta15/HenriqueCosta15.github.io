The AI TikTok Workflow

Here is how to use these tools to go from idea to posted video in under an hour:

Step 1 — Hook and script: Ask ChatGPT for 10 hook variations on your topic. Pick the strongest one. Have ChatGPT write the full 30 to 60 second script around it.

Step 2 — Voiceover: Paste the script into ElevenLabs. Generate the narration. Download the audio file.

Step 3 — Visuals: Record your own footage if you are on camera. For faceless content, generate clips in Runway or use stock footage. Synthesia if you want an avatar presenter.

Step 4 — Edit: Bring everything into CapCut. Drop the voiceover, sync visuals, add auto-captions, apply background music. Export in 9:16 vertical format.

Step 5 — Caption and post: ChatGPT writes your caption and hashtag set. Post directly from CapCut to TikTok.

Total time: 30 to 45 minutes per video once you have the workflow down. That is fast enough to post daily without burning out.

Faceless TikTok Stack

Want to build a TikTok account without ever appearing on camera? Here is the minimal setup: ChatGPT for scripts, ElevenLabs for voice, Runway for visuals, CapCut to assemble. Starting cost: zero. Everything listed above has a free tier. Once your account grows and monetizes, upgrade ElevenLabs to the $5/month Starter plan for more voice minutes.
