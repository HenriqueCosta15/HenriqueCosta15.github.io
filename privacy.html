Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyAIToolPick ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you visit myaitoolpick.com ("the Site"). We are committed to compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable EU/Portuguese data protection law.

1. Data Controller

MyAIToolPick is operated from Portugal. For data protection queries, contact us at: hello@aitoolpick.com

2. What Data We Collect

Analytics Data (with your consent): If you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics to collect anonymized usage data — pages visited, time on site, approximate geographic region, device type, and referral source. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

Affiliate Tracking (with your consent): If you click an affiliate link, third-party affiliate networks may set cookies to track conversions. These are governed by the affiliate network's own privacy policies.

Contact Data: If you email us, we retain your email address and message content to respond to your query. We do not add you to any mailing list without explicit consent.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): Analytics and affiliate tracking cookies are only placed after you provide explicit consent via our cookie banner.

Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)): Processing email correspondence to respond to queries you initiate.

4. Cookies

We use cookies only with your consent (except strictly necessary cookies). See our Cookie Policy for full details. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your cookies and declining on next visit.

5. Data Retention

Analytics data is retained for 26 months (Google Analytics default). Email correspondence is retained for as long as necessary to resolve your query, then deleted. Cookie consent records are retained for 12 months.

6. Data Sharing

We share anonymized analytics data with Google (Analytics). We do not sell your personal data. Affiliate networks receive only the data necessary to track commissions (click IDs, not personal identifiers). All third-party processors operate under GDPR-compliant data processing agreements.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR

As an EU resident, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction — limit how we process your data
  • Data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, without affecting lawfulness of prior processing

To exercise any right, email hello@aitoolpick.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD) at cnpd.pt.

8. International Transfers

Google Analytics may transfer data outside the EU/EEA. Google operates under Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, providing adequate protection for such transfers.

9. Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted by updating the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

For any privacy-related queries: hello@aitoolpick.com
