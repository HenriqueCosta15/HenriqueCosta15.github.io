Descript is the single most useful AI tool for YouTubers. It transcribes your footage, then lets you edit video by editing the text. Delete a sentence from the transcript and it cuts that clip from the video. The AI filler word removal is what most creators come for: Descript scans your recording for every "um", "uh", "like", and "you know" and removes them in one click. It also generates captions automatically and lets you clone your voice so you can fix mistakes by typing new text instead of re-recording. Nothing saves as much time in the editing phase.

Best for: All YouTubers, especially talking-head and interview content

The Full AI YouTube Workflow

Here is how to chain these tools together for a complete video from idea to upload:

Step 1 — Script: Use ChatGPT to draft the script. Prompt it with your topic, target length, and tone. Edit the output until it sounds like you.

Step 2 — Voiceover: Paste the finished script into ElevenLabs or Murf AI and generate the narration. Download the audio file.

Step 3 — Visuals: Record your own footage if you are on camera. For faceless content, use Synthesia for a presenter avatar or Runway for generated B-roll clips. Stock footage sites fill the gaps.

Step 4 — Edit: Bring everything into Descript. Drop the voiceover, sync it to the transcript, cut any dead air, and add captions. For Shorts, use CapCut instead.

Step 5 — Publish: Export, upload to YouTube, use ChatGPT to write the description and tags.

A video that once took a full day can be done in 2 to 3 hours using this stack. The free tiers of Descript, ElevenLabs, CapCut, and ChatGPT cover everything a starting channel needs before spending a dollar.

Faceless Channel Stack

If you want to build a YouTube channel without ever appearing on camera, here is the minimal stack:

ElevenLabs for narration. Synthesia if you want a presenter avatar. Runway or free stock footage for visuals. Descript to assemble and add captions. ChatGPT to script everything. Monthly cost starting at around $50 all-in once you need paid plans, which is nothing compared to hiring a production team.
