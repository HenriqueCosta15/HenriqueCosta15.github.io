What Is Grammarly?

Grammarly started as a grammar and spell-checker but has transformed into a full AI writing assistant. With over 30 million daily active users, it's the most widely used writing tool on the internet: installed as a browser extension, desktop app, and mobile keyboard.

In 2026, Grammarly's AI features rival dedicated AI writing tools. It doesn't just catch mistakes. It rewrites, improves tone, adjusts formality, and now generates full drafts from prompts.

Key Features

Grammar and Spell Check

Still the strongest part of the product. Grammarly catches errors that Microsoft Word misses: comma splices, misplaced modifiers, subject-verb disagreements in complex sentences. The free plan covers this comprehensively.

Tone Detection

Grammarly analyzes the emotional tone of your writing and suggests adjustments. Write something that comes across as aggressive when you meant to be assertive? It flags it and offers alternatives. Surprisingly useful for professional emails.

AI Rewriting

Highlight any sentence or paragraph and ask Grammarly to rewrite it: more concisely, more formally, more confidently. The output is consistently better than the original. This alone saves significant editing time.

Plagiarism Detection

Premium users get access to plagiarism checking against billions of web pages. Essential for students, academics, and anyone publishing original content.

Grammarly GO (AI Generation)

The newer AI generation feature lets you generate emails, documents, and content from prompts. Not as specialized as Jasper or Writesonic, but convenient for quick tasks without switching apps.

Free vs Premium: Is It Worth Upgrading?

The free plan handles grammar, spelling, and basic style suggestions, and covers most casual and occasional writing needs. Premium unlocks tone adjustments, advanced clarity rewrites, full AI generation, plagiarism detection, and word choice improvements. For anyone writing professionally and publishing regularly, the annual Premium plan at $12/month makes sense. The $30/month rate is harder to justify when ChatGPT or Claude can handle rewrites for a similar price with more flexibility.

Our Verdict

Grammarly works well as a polish layer on top of anything you write. The free tier handles grammar, spelling, and basic clarity suggestions, which covers the majority of what most people actually need from it. The Premium plan at $30/month (or $12/month billed annually) unlocks tone adjustments, advanced rewrites, and plagiarism detection. That annual price is reasonable for frequent writers. The monthly rate is hard to justify unless you're writing constantly.

One limitation worth noting: Grammarly sometimes over-corrects and flattens your voice. Accept suggestions selectively rather than accepting all.
