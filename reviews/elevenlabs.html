What Is ElevenLabs?

ElevenLabs is an AI voice generation platform that converts text to speech with remarkable realism. Founded in 2022, it quickly became the industry standard — used by major media companies, game developers, content creators, and enterprises worldwide.

Its key breakthrough is emotional intelligence in voice output. Unlike robotic text-to-speech tools of the past, ElevenLabs voices convey natural pauses, emphasis, emotion, and rhythm that makes content genuinely engaging.

Key Features

Text-to-Speech

The core feature and the best in the industry. Paste any text and choose from hundreds of voices — the output sounds like a professional voice actor, not a robot. Accents, languages (29+), pacing, and tone are all adjustable.

Voice Cloning

Upload a one-minute audio sample and ElevenLabs creates a digital clone of that voice. The clone captures nuances that basic TTS misses — breathing patterns, natural inflections, speaking pace. Professional-grade results from minimal input.

AI Voice Design

Can't find the right voice? Describe what you want — "deep, authoritative American male in his 40s" — and ElevenLabs generates a new voice matching your description. No recording required.

Projects (Long-Form Audio)

The Projects feature handles full audiobooks, long podcasts, and multi-chapter content. Auto-assigns different voices to different characters, maintains consistency throughout, and exports cleanly.
