What Is Runway?

Runway is a creative platform built for video — specifically for generating and editing it in ways that used to require a full production team. Founded in 2018 and based in New York, it's become the tool that professional filmmakers, YouTube creators, and studios turn to when they want to do something that traditional software can't.

The big feature is video generation from a text prompt or an image. Type in a description of a scene and Runway produces a short video clip. It's not perfect, but the quality from Gen-3 Alpha is genuinely impressive — motion is smoother, detail is sharper, and results look more cinematic than anything from earlier models.

Key Features

Gen-3 Alpha (Text to Video)

The headline feature. You write a prompt describing a scene — lighting, movement, mood, camera angle — and Runway generates a 5 or 10 second video clip. The output quality varies depending on how specific your prompt is, but with good prompting the results are striking. Consistent characters across clips is still a challenge, but Runway is improving faster than anyone else in this space.

Image to Video

Upload a still image and Runway animates it. This is one of the more reliable features — you start with something you know looks right and let Runway add motion to it. Great for turning product photos into short ads, animating portraits, or creating movement from concept art.

Motion Brush

Paint over specific parts of an image and tell Runway how to move them — left, right, zoom, rotate. It gives you directional control over the animation without needing to write a complex prompt. One of the more intuitive tools in the suite once you get used to it.

Video Editing Tools

Beyond generation, Runway has a growing set of editing tools: background removal, inpainting (removing objects from video), frame interpolation for smoother slow motion, and more. These sit alongside the generation features in a browser-based editor that's clean and reasonably fast to navigate.

Act One

A newer feature that lets you capture facial expressions from a webcam or video clip and transfer them to a generated character. Early days, but it points to where Runway is heading — full character control for creators who want consistent personas in their videos.

How Much Does It Actually Cost Per Video?

A 5-second Gen-3 Alpha clip costs around 50 credits. On the Standard plan at $15/month that's roughly 12 clips per month — not a lot if you're experimenting. Pro at $35/month gets you around 45 clips. For creators who are publishing regularly, the Unlimited plan makes more financial sense despite the watermark (which can be removed on Enterprise).

Who Is Runway Best For?

Runway is for people who create video content professionally or seriously. YouTubers, short filmmakers, social media creators, and marketing teams who need video assets will get the most out of it. If you just want to experiment with video generation occasionally, the free credits are enough to see what it can do. But if you're thinking about using it as part of a real workflow, budget for at least the Pro plan.

Runway is the best video generation tool available in 2026, and the gap between it and the competition is meaningful. The results from Gen-3 Alpha are cinematic in a way that earlier models weren't. The pricing is real, the learning curve is real, but so are the results. If video is part of your work, Runway deserves a proper trial.
