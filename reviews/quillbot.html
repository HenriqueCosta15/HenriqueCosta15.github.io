What Is QuillBot?

QuillBot is a writing tool built around one core feature: rephrasing text. You paste something in, and it rewrites it — keeping the meaning but changing the words and structure. It launched in 2017 and has grown to over 35 million users, mostly students and content writers who need to reword text quickly.

Beyond paraphrasing, QuillBot has added a grammar checker, summarizer, citation generator, translator, and a plagiarism checker in recent years. It's basically become a writing assistant focused on reworking existing text rather than generating it from scratch.

Key Features

Paraphraser

This is QuillBot's bread and butter. Paste in your text and choose a mode — Standard, Fluency, Formal, Academic, Simple, Creative, or Expand/Shorten. Each mode shifts the output in a different direction. The Formal and Academic modes are genuinely useful for students who need to rephrase source material without just copying it. The output quality is consistently solid, though you'll occasionally need to tweak a word or two.

Grammar Checker

QuillBot's grammar checker catches most of the common mistakes — tense errors, subject-verb agreement, punctuation issues. It's not quite as thorough as Grammarly, but it handles the basics well and it's free to use with no word limit. Good enough for a quick pass before submitting something.

Summarizer

Paste in an article, research paper, or long document and QuillBot will condense it into the key points. You can choose between a paragraph summary or bullet points. For students working through dense reading lists, this is a real time-saver. The accuracy depends on the source — well-structured text summarizes cleanly, but messier writing sometimes loses important context.

Citation Generator

Supports APA, MLA, Chicago, and other common formats. You paste in a URL or DOI and it generates the citation automatically. It's not perfect — you should always double-check the output — but it gets you 90% of the way there in seconds. Students will use this constantly.

Plagiarism Checker

Available on Premium plans. Checks your text against online sources and flags any close matches. Useful for making sure your paraphrased content is different enough from the original. The results are decent but not as comprehensive as dedicated plagiarism tools like Turnitin.

Free vs Premium: Is It Worth Upgrading?

The free plan works fine for short bursts — 125 words per paraphrase is enough for a sentence or two, but frustrating for longer passages. Premium removes that limit, unlocks all paraphrasing modes, adds the plagiarism checker, and lets you paraphrase up to 10,000 words at once. For students writing research papers, the annual Premium plan at around $10/month is genuinely worth it just for the word limit removal alone.

Who Is QuillBot Best For?

QuillBot is built for people who are working with existing text — not people who need to generate content from scratch. If you're a student paraphrasing sources, a writer reworking drafts, or a non-native English speaker trying to smooth out your writing, QuillBot is the right tool. If you need to generate articles or marketing copy from a prompt, look at Jasper or Writesonic instead.

Our Verdict

QuillBot is the best paraphrasing tool available, and it's not particularly close. The free plan is generous enough to test it properly, and the Premium upgrade is affordable for what you get. It's not trying to be everything — it's trying to be the best at rewording text, and it largely succeeds.
