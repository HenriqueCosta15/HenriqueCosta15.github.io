How to Build a Sales AI Stack That Actually Saves Time

The trap with sales AI is using too many tools and spending more time switching between them than you save. The most effective setup uses two to three tools that cover the whole cycle:

Before the call: Perplexity for 5-minute prospect research. Use it to find one specific insight about the company to open with. This takes less time than LinkedIn and returns better information.

Writing outreach: Rytr for volume outbound sequences. ChatGPT for key accounts that warrant a more personalized approach. Grammarly running in the background on everything.

During the call: Otter AI running on every call. Stop taking notes. Focus on the conversation. Read the summary after.

After the call: Otter's auto-generated follow-up draft as the base. QuillBot to tighten the proposal when the deal progresses.

Total cost under $60/month. Time saved per rep: 8 to 12 hours per week on average. That time goes back into active selling.
