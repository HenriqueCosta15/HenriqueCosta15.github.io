Frequently Asked Questions

Which AI tools have a completely free plan?

ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity AI, Grammarly, Canva, QuillBot, Rytr, ElevenLabs, Otter.ai, and Notion AI all have permanent free plans. ChatGPT gives you GPT-4o with daily limits. Gemini is unlimited on the free tier. Rytr gives you 10,000 characters per month. ElevenLabs gives you 10,000 characters of audio per month. These are genuine free plans, not trials.

What is the cheapest paid AI tool?

ElevenLabs starts at $5 per month, making it the cheapest paid AI tool with meaningful features. Rytr starts at $9 per month for unlimited character generation. QuillBot starts at $9.95 per month with no word limits on paraphrasing. Midjourney starts at $10 per month. For general AI assistants, Notion AI is $10 per month as an add-on to an existing workspace.

Do AI tools offer student discounts?

Yes. GitHub Copilot is free for verified students through GitHub Education. Grammarly offers discounts for educational institutions. Notion offers a free Personal Pro plan for students and educators with a valid education email. Always check the tool's pricing page for current educational offers, as these change regularly.
