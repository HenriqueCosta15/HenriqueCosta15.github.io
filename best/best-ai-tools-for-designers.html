8 Best AI Tools for Designers in 2026

AI has not replaced designers. It has split them into two groups: those who use it and move twice as fast, and those who do not. Here are the tools the fastest designers are using right now.

  2. Canva AI: Best for fast production of design assets
  3. Adobe Firefly: Best for designers already in the Creative Suite
  4. ChatGPT: Best for writing copy, briefs, and client proposals
  5. Runway: Best for motion and video work
  6. Murf AI: Best for voiceover on design presentation videos
  7. QuillBot: Best for polishing client-facing written deliverables

The design profession has not disappeared. It has accelerated. The tools that used to take a week of iteration now take an afternoon. The designers who embrace this are billing more per project and taking on more clients. The ones resisting it are being undercut on both speed and price.

How to Add AI to Your Design Workflow

The designers getting the most value from AI use it at specific stages rather than everywhere at once:

Discovery and concept: Use Midjourney or Adobe Firefly to generate 20 to 30 visual directions in an afternoon. Present the strongest three to a client as a mood board. This replaces days of manual mood board curation.

Copy and briefs: Use ChatGPT to write the creative brief, presentation narrative, and all the text copy in the design. No more blank page on the proposal document.

Production: Use Canva AI for template-based deliverables the client will maintain themselves. Use Adobe Firefly inside Photoshop for precise image editing tasks.

Presentation and delivery: Use Murf or ElevenLabs to narrate recorded walkthroughs. Use Runway to add motion to static assets when clients ask for animated versions.

The result is a workflow where a solo designer can produce the output of a two or three-person team and bill accordingly.
