What Is Google Gemini?

Gemini is Google's AI assistant, built on their Gemini family of models. It replaced Google Bard in 2024 and has since become a serious competitor to ChatGPT and Claude. Gemini is deeply integrated into Google's product suite — Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Drive, and Search — making it the natural choice for anyone living in the Google ecosystem.

The free version runs on Gemini 1.5 Flash, while Gemini Advanced (paid) uses the more powerful Gemini 1.5 Pro model with significantly better reasoning and longer context handling.

Key Features

Google Workspace Integration

Gemini's biggest differentiator. It works directly inside Gmail (drafting and summarizing emails), Google Docs (writing and editing), Sheets (formula help and data analysis), and Drive (searching and summarizing documents). If you spend your workday in Google's tools, this integration alone justifies the subscription.

Multimodal Capabilities

Gemini handles text, images, audio, and video natively. Upload an image and ask questions about it, have it analyze a chart, or describe what's in a photo. The multimodal handling is among the best available and reflects Google's strength in computer vision research.

Real-Time Google Search

Gemini pulls live information from Google Search by default. Unlike ChatGPT which added browsing as a feature, search integration is baked into Gemini's core — making it consistently reliable for current events, prices, and up-to-date facts.

Long Context Window

Gemini 1.5 Pro supports up to 1 million tokens of context — the largest of any mainstream AI. This means you can upload entire books, lengthy codebases, or hours of video and have Gemini reason across all of it. For research-heavy tasks this is a significant practical advantage.

Gemini for Mobile

On Android, Gemini can replace Google Assistant entirely — answering questions, controlling apps, and acting as a voice assistant. The mobile experience is polished and well-integrated into Android's ecosystem.

Gemini vs The Competition

Against ChatGPT, Gemini is close but slightly behind on writing quality and the breadth of its feature ecosystem. ChatGPT's custom GPTs and DALL-E image generation give it an edge for most general users. However, Gemini's Google integration makes it the clear winner for anyone working heavily in Gmail and Docs.

Against Claude, Gemini wins on real-time information access and Google integration. Claude wins on writing quality, nuance, and accuracy on complex tasks.

Our Verdict

Gemini Advanced is a strong AI assistant that earns its place among the top tier. If you're a Google Workspace user — Gmail, Docs, Drive — it's genuinely the best option because the integration makes your existing tools significantly more powerful. For users outside the Google ecosystem, ChatGPT or Claude are likely better fits.

The free plan is worth trying regardless. Google Search integration alone makes it a useful tool for research and current information.
