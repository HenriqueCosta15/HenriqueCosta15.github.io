What Is Claude?

Claude is an AI assistant developed by Anthropic, an AI safety company founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers. Anthropic's focus on safety and reliability directly shapes Claude's behavior — it's more careful, more honest about uncertainty, and less likely to confidently hallucinate than most competitors.

In 2026, Claude is available in several versions: Claude Haiku (fast and lightweight), Claude Sonnet (balanced performance), and Claude Opus (most capable). The free tier provides access to Sonnet, while the Pro plan ($20/month) unlocks Opus and significantly higher usage limits.

Key Features

200,000-Token Context Window

Claude's context window is one of the largest available — 200,000 tokens translates to roughly 150,000 words, or an entire novel. You can paste a full research paper, lengthy contract, or entire codebase and ask questions about it in a single session. This is genuinely useful in ways that smaller context windows aren't.

Writing Quality

Claude's writing is the most natural-sounding AI output we've encountered. It avoids the telltale patterns of AI writing — repetitive sentence structure, overuse of certain phrases, unnatural transitions. When we ran blind tests with experienced editors, Claude's output was the hardest to identify as AI-generated. For any writing task requiring a human feel, Claude leads the field.

Accuracy and Honesty

Claude is notably more likely to say "I'm not certain" rather than confidently stating incorrect information. In our fact-checking tests, Claude had the lowest hallucination rate of any AI assistant we tested. For research and analysis tasks where accuracy matters, this is a significant advantage.

Document Analysis

Upload PDFs, paste long documents, or share large amounts of text — Claude processes and analyzes them with remarkable comprehension. Summarizing lengthy reports, extracting key points from legal documents, or answering questions about dense academic papers are tasks Claude handles exceptionally well.

Coding Assistance

Claude writes clean, well-commented code with thorough explanations. It's particularly good at explaining what code does in plain language — useful for non-developers who need to understand technical implementations. Debugging sessions with Claude tend to resolve faster than with competitors because it asks clarifying questions rather than guessing.

Claude Free vs Claude Pro

The free tier gives you access to Claude Sonnet with daily usage limits. For casual users and students, the free plan is genuinely useful — limits are hit only with heavy daily use. Claude Pro at $20/month provides access to Claude Opus (significantly more capable), 5x more usage, priority access during peak times, and early access to new features. For professionals using Claude daily, Pro pays for itself quickly.
