The AI Email Marketing Workflow

Plan: ChatGPT outlines the sequence, the angle for each email, and generates 10 subject line options per email. Pick the strongest two to A/B test.

Write: Rytr drafts each email body from the subject line and campaign brief. 3 to 5 minutes per email.

Edit: QuillBot tightens the copy. Grammarly catches errors. You add any personal details, specific proof points, or brand-specific language that AI cannot know.

Send: Load into Klaviyo or Mailchimp. Use platform AI for send time optimization. Schedule.

A 5-email welcome sequence that used to take a full day can be written, edited, and scheduled in under 2 hours with this workflow.

One Rule for AI Email Copy

Always read the email aloud before sending. AI copy often sounds fine when read silently but awkward when spoken. If you would not say it in a conversation with a customer, edit it. The goal is email that sounds like a person wrote it because a person who cares about the reader, not a system optimizing for clicks.
