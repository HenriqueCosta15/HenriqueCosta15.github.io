FAQ

Common Questions

How can personal trainers use AI?

Personal trainers are using AI in three main areas: program creation (ChatGPT for customized workout and nutrition frameworks), content marketing (Rytr and Canva AI for social media and newsletters), and digital product development (Synthesia and ElevenLabs for video and audio course content). The trainers getting the most value are those using AI to build scalable online programs that generate revenue beyond their one-on-one client hours.

Can AI write workout programs?

ChatGPT can generate structured workout programs from a brief describing a client's goals, experience level, available equipment, and training frequency. Most trainers use it as a starting framework that they then customize based on their professional knowledge and the client's specific needs. It saves significant time on program design without removing the trainer's expertise from the final product.

What is the best AI tool for fitness content creation?

Rytr is the fastest tool for producing consistent written content: social captions, email newsletters, and promotional copy. Canva AI handles the visual side. For video and audio content, ElevenLabs adds professional voiceover and Synthesia creates presenter-style videos. Most successful fitness content creators use a combination of all four.
