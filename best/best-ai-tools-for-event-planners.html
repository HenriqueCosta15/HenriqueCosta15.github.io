How Event Planners Use AI in Practice

The planners saving the most time with AI use it on the document and communication layer, not the logistics layer.

Proposals and RFPs: ChatGPT takes an event brief and outputs a structured proposal in minutes. You customize the venue names, vendor specifics, and pricing. The structure and boilerplate are done.

Project tracking: Notion AI keeps all vendor contacts, timelines, budgets, and checklists in one place. When a planning call produces a long list of action items, Notion summarizes and organizes them.

Vendor emails: QuillBot adapts a base email template for each vendor type without full rewrites. One template, 10 vendor-specific versions in a few minutes.

Marketing materials: Canva AI handles invitations, programs, and sponsor graphics. Rytr or ChatGPT handles the promotional copy. Both together replace what used to require a freelance designer and copywriter.

What AI Cannot Replace in Event Planning

AI cannot manage vendor relationships built on years of trust. It cannot solve on-site problems when the AV setup fails an hour before doors open. It cannot read a client's body language in a site visit and adjust the pitch accordingly. The judgment calls that determine whether an event actually succeeds still belong to the planner. AI removes the document overhead so you have more time for those calls.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can AI help write event proposals?

Yes. ChatGPT is particularly useful for drafting event proposals and RFP responses. Give it the event brief, budget range, and client goals, and ask it to structure a professional proposal. You review and customize the specifics -- venue names, vendor quotes, personal recommendations -- but the document structure, section headers, and boilerplate language get done in minutes instead of hours.

Which AI tool helps most with event logistics?

Notion AI is the most useful for the logistics side. It keeps all planning documents, timelines, vendor contacts, and checklists in one place and helps you summarize and organize the information. For communication volume, ChatGPT and QuillBot handle the email and document drafting that logistics coordination requires.

How do event planners use AI for marketing?

Canva AI handles the visual side: invitations, event programs, sponsor graphics, and social promotion materials. Rytr handles the copy side: promotional emails, event descriptions, and social media posts. Most event planners use both in combination, with Canva for design and Rytr or ChatGPT for the text.
