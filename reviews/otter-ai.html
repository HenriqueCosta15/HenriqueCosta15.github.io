What Is Otter.ai?

Otter.ai is an AI tool that joins your online meetings, records them, transcribes everything that's said, and then generates a summary with action items. It works with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. You set it up once and from then on, it handles note-taking automatically.

It's been around since 2018 and has had time to get the transcription accuracy right. The AI summary features were added more recently and have improved a lot over the past couple of years.

Who Should Use Otter.ai?

If you spend a meaningful amount of time in meetings, Otter.ai is worth looking at. It's especially useful for:

  • Professionals who have back-to-back calls and struggle to keep track of what was discussed
  • Managers who need to share meeting notes with people who weren't there
  • Journalists and researchers conducting interviews
  • Salespeople who want a record of client calls
  • Students recording lectures for review later

If you rarely have meetings, the free plan is more than enough and you may not need to pay anything.

Key Features

Automatic Meeting Transcription

Otter joins your calls as a participant and transcribes everything in real time. You can follow along live as the transcript appears on screen, or just ignore it and read it afterward. Speaker identification works well in most cases, labeling who said what throughout the transcript.

AI Meeting Summary

After the call ends, Otter generates a summary. Not just bullet points — it pulls out the key topics discussed, decisions made, and action items assigned. The quality is good enough that you can send it directly to teammates without editing most of the time.

OtterPilot (Automatic Join)

Connect your Google or Outlook calendar and Otter joins every meeting automatically. You don't have to remember to start it or invite it. It just shows up. This is the feature that makes it genuinely hands-off once you've set it up.

Ask Otter

After a meeting, you can ask questions about what was discussed. "What did Sarah say about the budget?" or "What are the next steps?" It searches the transcript and gives you a direct answer. Useful for long meetings where you need to find something specific without scrolling through pages of transcript.

Integrations

Otter connects to Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, and Notion. You can automatically push meeting summaries to your CRM or send notes to a Slack channel after every call.

Transcription Accuracy

This is where Otter does well and where it has limitations. For clear audio with native English speakers, accuracy is high — around 90-95% in our testing. You'll see occasional wrong words but the meaning is always clear.

Where it struggles: heavy accents, people talking over each other, poor audio quality, and technical jargon it hasn't seen before. If your meetings involve participants with strong accents, expect to do more cleanup on the transcript.
