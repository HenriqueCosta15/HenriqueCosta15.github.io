What Is Notion AI?

Notion AI is an add-on to the Notion workspace app. It brings AI features directly into your notes, docs, and databases — so instead of copying text into ChatGPT and back, you can just highlight something in Notion and ask AI to work on it right there.

It launched in 2023 and has been updated consistently since. The AI itself is powered by a mix of models (not proprietary), but what makes it useful is how naturally it fits into the Notion workflow rather than any special model capability.

Who Should Use Notion AI?

The answer is simple: people who already use Notion. If you manage your notes, projects, or documents in Notion, the AI features slot in without any extra effort. If you don't use Notion, starting just for the AI makes no sense when dedicated writing tools like Claude or Jasper do it better.

  • Teams using Notion for project management and docs
  • Solo creators who keep everything in Notion
  • Students who take notes in Notion and want help studying

Key Features

Write and Edit Inside Your Docs

Highlight any text in Notion and Notion AI can rewrite it, make it shorter, change the tone, fix grammar, or translate it. You can also ask it to continue writing from where you left off. It works directly in the editor so you stay in one place the whole time.

Summarize Long Pages

This is one of the most practical features. Got a 20-page meeting notes document? Ask Notion AI to summarize it and you'll have a readable overview in seconds. Works well on meeting notes, research dumps, and long project docs.

Ask Questions About Your Workspace

This is where Notion AI gets genuinely impressive. You can ask it questions like "What were the action items from last week's standup?" or "What did we decide about the product launch?" and it searches across your workspace to find the answer. For teams with a lot of documentation, this saves real time.

Action Items from Meeting Notes

Paste in raw meeting notes and Notion AI will pull out the tasks and next steps automatically. Saves the five to ten minutes you'd usually spend reading back through the notes looking for what you promised to do.

Our Verdict

Notion AI does exactly what it's supposed to. It's not trying to be the most powerful AI writer — it's trying to be the most useful AI for people who already live inside Notion. On that measure, it succeeds.

The Q&A feature that searches your workspace is the standout. If your team stores information in Notion, being able to ask plain-language questions across all those pages is a real time-saver. The summarize and edit features are solid but not remarkable.

At $10/month on top of your Notion plan, it's worth it if you use Notion daily. Don't sign up for Notion just to try the AI — there are better dedicated options for that.
