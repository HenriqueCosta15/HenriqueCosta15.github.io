What Is Surfer SEO?

Surfer SEO is an AI-powered content optimization platform that helps you write articles designed to rank on Google. Instead of guessing what Google wants, Surfer analyzes the top 20 results for any keyword and extracts the patterns — word count, keyword density, headings, structure — that winning content shares.

The result is a data-driven content brief and real-time editor that scores your content as you write, telling you exactly what to add, remove, or adjust to improve your ranking potential.

Key Features

Content Editor

The core feature. Write your article directly in Surfer's editor and receive a real-time content score (0-100) based on how well you match the patterns of top-ranking pages. The editor highlights missing keywords, suggests heading structures, and shows ideal word count. Genuinely useful guidance, not generic advice.

AI Article Writer

Surfer now includes an AI writer that generates SEO-optimized articles using its own data. The output is already structured to rank — headings, keyword placement, and word count are all optimized before you start editing.

Keyword Research

Enter a seed keyword and Surfer surfaces related terms with search volume, keyword difficulty, and topical clusters. Helps you identify which keywords to target and how to group content for maximum authority.

SERP Analyzer

Deep analysis of search results for any keyword — what content types rank, what questions are answered, what backlink profiles look like. Essential competitive intelligence for content planning.
