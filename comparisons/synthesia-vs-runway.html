The Core Difference

Synthesia and Runway are both AI video tools, but they solve completely different problems. Synthesia is a business video platform: you write a script, choose an AI avatar, and it renders a professional video with a digital presenter reading your content. The output looks like a polished training video or explainer. Runway is a creative AI video studio: it generates video footage from text prompts, edits existing footage with AI tools like background removal and inpainting, and helps filmmakers and creators produce cinematic content.

Synthesia is designed for businesses producing communications at scale. Runway is designed for creators and filmmakers who want to push what video can look like. Knowing which camp you fall into makes the choice straightforward.

Synthesia: What It Does Well

Synthesia's strongest selling point is the speed from idea to finished video. Write a script, select an avatar from 230 or more options, add slides or media, and export. A five-minute training video can be ready in under an hour without a camera, microphone, actor, or editing software. For organizations that need to produce video content regularly, that efficiency advantage compounds quickly.

The custom avatar feature is Synthesia's most powerful differentiator for enterprises. Record one session with an approved employee and Synthesia builds a digital avatar of that person that can present any future script in their voice and likeness. This is how large companies maintain a consistent on-camera presence without booking studio time for every new piece of content.

The 140-language support is also unmatched in the avatar video category. Translate your training content into any major language and the avatar delivers it in the local language with correct lip sync. For global organizations, this eliminates the localization bottleneck that makes multilingual video production prohibitively expensive.

Runway: What It Does Well

Runway's text-to-video generation is genuinely impressive for creative work. Describe a scene and the Gen-3 Alpha model renders a short video clip with real camera movement, lighting, and cinematic quality. The results are used in music videos, short films, social media content, and experimental projects where the goal is to create footage that would be difficult or expensive to film in the real world.

Beyond generation, Runway has a full suite of AI editing tools that give creators capabilities that used to require years of After Effects expertise. Remove the background from any video, erase unwanted objects from footage, track motion for visual effects, and interpolate frames for smoother slow motion. These tools make it possible to polish footage quickly in ways that would have taken hours before AI.

For YouTubers and content creators, Runway's combination of generation and editing makes it the most versatile creative AI video platform available. The learning curve is steeper than Synthesia because there are more options, but users who invest the time get significantly more creative control over their output.

Runway is cheaper at the entry level. However, Synthesia's Starter plan at $29/month gives 10 complete videos per month with no per-video credit system. Runway's credit system means heavy users can burn through their allocation quickly on longer or more complex generations. For teams producing a predictable volume of videos, Synthesia's flat per-video pricing is easier to budget.

When to Use Synthesia

Choose Synthesia if:

You need to produce professional presenter videos for training, internal communications, onboarding, or marketing without filming anyone. Synthesia is the right tool for organizations that need video at scale in multiple languages with a consistent branded presenter. The workflow is straightforward enough that non-technical team members can produce finished videos without creative training.

When to Use Runway

Choose Runway if:

You are a filmmaker, YouTuber, creative agency, or content creator who wants to generate or edit video footage with AI tools. Runway is for creative work: music videos, short films, experimental content, social media production, and any project where the goal is visual creativity rather than straightforward business communication. If you want to edit footage you've already shot, Runway's AI editing tools are also the most capable available.

The Verdict

These tools are not competing for the same users. Synthesia wins decisively for business video production: faster to produce, easier to scale, better suited for teams. Runway wins for creative and cinematic work: more visual freedom, better generation quality, more editing power.

If you work in L and D, HR, marketing, or any function where you produce business video regularly, start with Synthesia. The free trial shows you quickly whether the avatar video format works for your use case. If you are a creator or filmmaker looking to push the boundaries of what AI can do visually, Runway is the more powerful creative tool.
